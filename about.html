Our Story

How a Small Book Nook Became a Community

Nookly started in a cozy corner of a neighborhood café where a few parents gathered to read with their children. What began as a weekly storytelling circle quickly grew into a vibrant club with hundreds of families, dedicated educators, and a shared mission to make reading an adventure.

Today, Nookly is a warm, inclusive space where children aged 4–12 explore new worlds through books, workshops, and events — all while building confidence and a lifelong love for learning.

2021 Café Story Corner
