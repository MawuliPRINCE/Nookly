Weekly Sessions

Saturday Reading Meetups

Every Saturday morning, our club members gather in small groups (max 8 kids) led by a trained story guide. We read a chapter together, talk about characters, predict what happens next, and then do a creative activity — drawing, acting, or building something from the story.

Groups are split by age: 4–6, 7–9, and 10–12, so every child feels comfortable and engaged.
